# User Personas
| Persona | Needs | Expertise | Pressure | Design response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Doctor (Dr. Sharma) | Rapid patient info | High | High | Urgent-first list, global search |
| Nurse (Priya) | Monitor many patients, repetitive tasks | High | High | Ward grid with status icons, emergency alerts |
| Receptionist (Kavya) | Registration, booking, queue | Medium | High frequency | Quick actions, inline validation |
| Patient (Rahul) | Simple, reassuring information | Low–medium, anxious | Low | Plain language queue status, large targets |
