# Usability Testing Plan
**Participants:** 5 (mix of students/staff). **Tasks:** 1 find report, 2 book appointment, 3 check queue position, 4 respond to emergency alert.
**Measures:** completion, time, errors, interactions, satisfaction (SUS-style 1–5).
**Method:** think-aloud, 1 facilitator, tasks given as scenarios.
